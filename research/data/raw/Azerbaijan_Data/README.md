# Azerbaijan income, spending and inflation data

Collected 21 October 2026. Source: State Statistical Committee of the Republic of Azerbaijan (AZSTAT / stat.gov.az).

## Start here

Open SOURCE_INDEX.csv in Excel. Each row gives the file, subject, coverage, original download URL and download status. Raw source files are unchanged; only filenames have been renamed. Yearbooks use publication year, which is NOT the observation year.

## Folders

- 01_Household_Income_and_Spending: household income, expenditure, spending shares and demographic/economic breakdowns.
- 02_Prices_and_Inflation: national, monthly, category and regional CPI; average prices.
- 03_Yearbooks: complete official household and price publications, including methodology. The 2024 household publication is supplied in its original ZIP.
- 04_Price_Bulletins: historical annual and recent monthly price releases, in original Excel/PDF formats where available.

## What the data represent

Income: resources received per person, usually manat per month; check the source definition and included components.
Consumption: spending per person, usually manat per month.
Spending shares: percentage of total consumption allocated to categories. Use compatible category CPI to estimate group inflation exposure.
Income quintiles: five groups ranked by income. Expenditure quintiles: five groups ranked by spending. Do not equate them.
Deciles: ten ranked groups.
Urban/rural: residence type. Economic regions: geographic regions, not income groups.
Household-head status: categories such as hired workers, self-employed, pensioners/beneficiaries and other, as defined by the source.
CPI previous year = 100: 105.6 means a 5.6 percent increase. Base-year CPI levels are different from inflation rates.
Monthly, same-month-last-year, year-to-date and December-to-December comparisons must remain separate.

## Coverage and gaps

This is a collection of downloaded official files, NOT a fully harmonized or complete 2000-2026 panel.
National annual CPI workbook includes 1993-2025. Historical household income and consumption workbooks include 2001, 2005, and 2008-2024: they omit 2000, 2002-2004 and 2006-2007.
Many household group workbooks contain 2024 only. Income and consumption urban/rural workbooks also show 2023 comparisons. The 2026 household yearbook supplies 2025 data and selected historical observations, not every year for every group.
Detailed category and regional CPI workbooks cover 2020-2025; other price workbooks have different periods shown in their filenames and index. Bulletins may extend historical coverage but have not been merged or harmonized.
2026 is incomplete. Monthly files are releases, and may contain year-to-date comparisons, not independent monthly inflation observations.
Do not fill missing years with invented values, duplicate a recent basket across history without disclosure, or treat missing data as zero.
To complete annual group histories, obtain older household tables or request them from AZSTAT. Official ASIS income table (2001-2025) is an additional manual-export source, not included as a completed export here:
https://azstat.gov.az/statHtml/statHtml.do?language=en&orgId=994&tblId=DT_CA_008
